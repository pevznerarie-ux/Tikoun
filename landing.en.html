How it works

Four steps, from prep to grading

The same tool supports you from the lesson all the way to the class summary.

Import your lesson

A PDF, photos of the pages, or pasted text. Mastery relies solely on your content.

Get your test

AI suggests the questions, the answer key, and the point scale, in three levels if you'd like. You can edit everything.

Print a sheet for each student

Each sheet carries a QR code that identifies the student and the page, with answer boxes.

Scan and approve

By phone or photocopier. Handwriting is read, mistakes are highlighted; you approve each grade.

Features

Everything you need around a test

Class view

Success rate per question and the most common mistakes: you instantly see what wasn't understood.

Individual remediation

For every student below average, a sheet targeted at their mistakes, printable and scannable.

Differentiated sheets

The same skill at three levels (Foundational, Standard, Advanced), assigned based on each student's level.

MCQs graded without AI

Checked boxes are read straight off the scan: fast, reliable, and cost-effective.

Progress for every student

Grade history by subject and a suggested report card comment, to review before using it.

Papers returned as PDF

Each student receives their graded paper: grade, comments, and corrections, ready to print or send.

All subjects

Math formulas, essays, translation, languages: the header and point scale adapt.

Practice sheetsPro

Online quizzes built from the lesson: each student practices with their own code, questions change on every attempt, and feedback is instant.

Your private space

Your classes, students, and papers are visible only to you.

The teacher decides

AI suggests, you approve

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do I need a scanner?

No. A phone photo is enough — the four QR codes let us straighten the sheet. You can also upload a photocopier PDF with all the papers for the class.

Can the AI make mistakes?

Yes, which is why every transcription and every grade stays editable, and a paper only counts once you've approved it.

Who sees my students' data?

Your space is private: your classes, students, and papers are not visible to other registered teachers.

What happens after the beta?

Plan pricing will be announced before the end of the beta. You won't be charged anything without your consent.

My school doesn't show up

You can add it manually with its mailing address. It will appear as "unverified," without limiting your use of Mastery.
