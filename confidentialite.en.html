Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short: Mastery never sells data, shows no ads and uses no advertising trackers. Student data is used only by the teacher, in their private workspace. Student names are removed before anything is sent to the AI.

1. Who is responsible?

  • Teacher data (account, school): the publisher of Mastery, {{EDITEUR}}, is the controller.
  • Student data: the school or teacher who enters it is the controller; Mastery acts as a processor (a “school official” under FERPA) and processes this data only on their instructions, to provide the service. A data processing agreement is available to schools on request.

Questions or requests: {{CONTACT}}.

No email address, date of birth or sensitive data is collected for students. Students don’t create accounts themselves.

3. Legal basis

  • Teachers: performance of the contract (terms of use).
  • Students: the school’s educational mission or the teacher’s legitimate interest in assessing and supporting students. The school informs families that the tool is used.
  • Security and logs: legitimate interest.
  • Improving handwriting reading: Mastery’s legitimate interest in reading papers reliably, with the right to object at any time (My account, or school leadership for the whole school).
  • Student space: each student only sees their own results, published by their teacher, using their personal code and last name.

4. Artificial intelligence

To read handwriting, grade and generate questions, Mastery sends the necessary elements to the artificial intelligence service of Anthropic (Claude models). To limit the data sent:

  • only the answer boxes are cropped and sent; when a whole page must be sent, the header with the student’s name is masked (even when the page could not be straightened);
  • students’ first and last names are automatically removed from text sent;
  • under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and keeps it only for a limited time.

Grades proposed by the AI are suggestions: the teacher checks and approves them. No decision is made automatically.

Improving handwriting reading (on by default, can be turned off). When a teacher corrects how a handwritten answer was read, Mastery keeps the image of the answer box (no name, no header), the text read by the AI and the text corrected by the teacher, without the student’s name, class, grade or school. Handwriting is still personal data: these samples are pseudonymized and used only to measure and improve how Mastery reads handwriting (for example by showing them to the AI as reading examples). They are not used to train Anthropic’s models, and are never sold or shared. Teachers can turn this off in My account, and school leadership for the whole school; deletion is available on request.

5. Recipients and subprocessors

  • The teacher who created the data, colleagues in their space if the school created shared accounts, and the school’s leadership if the teacher joined it in Mastery; the Mastery administrator for support and sign-up approval.
  • Hosting: {{HEBERGEUR}} · server region: {{REGION}}.
  • Artificial intelligence: Anthropic, PBC (USA).
  • Subscription payments: Stripe Payments Europe Ltd (Ireland) and Stripe, Inc. (USA); Mastery never sees card numbers.
  • Fonts and technical libraries: Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd) and jsDelivr; these services receive the browser’s IP address when pages load, without cookies.

When data is processed outside the European Union, transfers are covered by GDPR safeguards (EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies, or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses).

6. Retention

  • Account data and student data: as long as the account is active. Teachers can delete a student, a class or their whole account in Mastery at any time: deletion is immediate. An account closed on request is deleted within 30 days.
  • Scanned paper images: at most 13 months after upload, then deleted automatically.
  • Automatic backups: rolling 14 days.
  • Pseudonymized reading samples: at most 2 years, deleted on request.
  • Technical logs: at most 12 months (no email addresses).
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days without activity for teachers, at most 120 days for students.

7. Security

Encrypted connection (HTTPS), hashed passwords (scrypt), separated spaces between teachers and schools (including scanned images), blocking of repeated password attempts, usage limits against abuse, daily backup.

8. Cookies

Mastery only uses strictly necessary cookies: keeping the teacher or student signed in, and remembering the language. No advertising or analytics cookies, so no consent banner is needed.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and export your data. For a student’s data, the request goes to the school or teacher, and Mastery helps them respond. Parents may review their child’s records through the school. Write to {{CONTACT}}.

10. Children

Students may be minors, including children under 13. Their data is entered by their teacher for school purposes, with the school’s authorization; students only see their own quizzes and results, using the personal code given by their teacher.
